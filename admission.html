WHAT YOU NEED

Admission Requirements

Required Documents

  • Academic certificates or examination results
  • Birth certificate or relevant identification
  • Passport-size photographs
  • School transfer documents where applicable
  • Other documents requested by the school

Important Information

  • Admission is subject to the school's requirements.
  • Parents or guardians should follow the official admission instructions.
  • Admission requirements may change depending on the academic year.
