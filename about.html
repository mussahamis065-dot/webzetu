About Iyunga Secondary School

WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Iyunga Secondary School

Iyunga Secondary School is an educational institution dedicated to providing quality education and developing students academically, socially and morally.

The school provides an environment where students can acquire knowledge, develop talents, build confidence and prepare themselves for future opportunities.

Our Mission

To provide quality education and develop knowledgeable, responsible and skilled students who can contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

To become a centre of excellence in education by empowering students with knowledge, skills, discipline and strong values.

WHAT WE BELIEVE IN

Our Core Values

Excellence

We encourage students to always strive for excellence.

Respect

We promote respect among students, teachers and the community.

Integrity

We encourage honesty, responsibility and good character.

Innovation

We encourage creativity, curiosity and new ideas.

MESSAGE FROM SCHOOL LEADERSHIP

Head of School's Message

Welcome to Iyunga Secondary School. We are committed to creating an environment that supports academic achievement, discipline, leadership and personal development.

We believe that every student has unique abilities and potential. Our responsibility is to provide the guidance and opportunities necessary for students to discover and develop those abilities.

Together with parents, teachers and the wider community, we continue working towards building a better future for our students.
